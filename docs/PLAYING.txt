HIGHWAY 1: KALONA TO IOWA CITY (OR WELLMAN)
============================================

A homemade game about real Iowa highways out of Kalona: 15 miles of Highway 1 north to
Iowa City, or 7 miles of Highway 22 west to Wellman.


HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click HighwayOne.exe.
3. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC". That's because this is a
   homemade game without a paid code-signing certificate, not because anything is wrong.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only have to do this once per build.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
The title screen: Drive, Practice, Records, Settings or Quit. Then pick the route, a
car, a paint color and the conditions in the garage, and get there causing as much
chaos as you can. Both routes start at the four-way stop where IA 1 meets IA 22 in
Kalona; the clock and the scoring start when you cross the line.
  Highway 1   north, 15.4 miles, from the stop sign to the first stop light in Iowa City
              (Mormon Trek Blvd).
  Highway 22  west, 6.7 miles, from the middle of the intersection, over the English
              River and past Mid-Prairie High School to 8th Ave in downtown Wellman.
Your best score and best time are saved for each route, car and condition.


THE CARS
--------
1998 Ford Windstar  Heavy boat. Soft and top-heavy, so it leans hard; tough enough
                    to plow through anything. Hubcaps and the sliding door come off.
                    Points x1.2.
1997 Toyota Tercel  93 horsepower. Light, nimble, slow, and fragile, but the engine
                    never dies: it can't be totaled, it just sheds mirrors and
                    bumpers. Points x1.5.
1993 Acura Legend   Fastest and grippiest. The hood and trunk pop open when it's
                    beaten up. Points x1.0.

Points:
  Mailbox 50 · fence 25 · hay bale 100 (heavy: it hurts) · plowing corn 5 a meter
  Air time 100 per half second · near miss with oncoming traffic 250
  Squeezing past something in your lane with almost no room (a close pass) 100
  Passing a car 100 · a buggy 150 · a tractor 200 · a semi 300
  A deer in your path that you miss 100
  Losing a cop 1,000 · dragging the Washington County deputy over the county line 2,000

Chain things together within 4 seconds and the combo multiplier climbs to x5. When
the combo runs out, its points are banked and safe. A hard hit, the tow truck, or a
wreck throws away the combo you haven't banked yet.

Make it to the finish and your whole score is multiplied by 1.5, plus 20 points for
every second under par (15 minutes to Iowa City, 6:40 to Wellman). Every point is also
multiplied by your car's number.

The run is over if:
  - the car is totaled (watch the health bar; not the Tercel)
  - it's on its roof or side for 3 seconds
  - you hit an Amish buggy
  - you hit oncoming traffic head-on at speed
  - you get busted
Stuck in a ditch? T calls the tow truck: back on the road for 500 points.


THE CONDITIONS
--------------
Summer afternoon    Tall green corn, full grip.
Harvest dusk        Low sun, brown corn and cut fields. Combines (wider than a lane)
                    and grain trucks. More deer.
Night               Headlights only. Buggy lanterns, farm yard lights, three times
                    the deer.
Thunderstorm        Rain, slick roads, lightning, and gusts that shove tall cars
                    around (hold on to that Windstar).
Winter              Snow, whiteout fog, snowplows, and black ice on the bridges and
                    wherever trees shade the road. You can see the ice: it's the dark,
                    glassy patches. Ease off before you get there.


THE BOOST
---------
Near misses fill the boost meter (bottom right, over the health bar): an oncoming car
whistling past your mirror fills 40% of it, squeezing past something going your way
with almost no room fills a quarter. Hold Shift (or B on a controller) to burn it: a
hard shove and a much higher top speed, faster than any deputy. A full meter lasts
4 seconds. Save it for when you need it.


PRACTICE
--------
Practice from the title screen: same game, but you pick where to start (Kalona or any
mile up the road) and nothing's saved. Good for learning a stretch or a speed trap.


SETTINGS
--------
From the title screen or the pause menu. Saved as you change them.
  Graphics      Low for slow PCs (lower resolution, half the trees), Medium, or High
                (smoother edges, more detail in the distance)
  Resolution    50-100%: lower it if the game stutters
  Scenery       everything, half the trees, or none (F2 does this while driving too)
  Display       a window or full screen
  Volume        overall, the car and effects, and the radio, separately
  Controller rumble  on or off


THE HORN
--------
H (or RB on a controller). Every car has its own (the Tercel goes "meep"). Lean on it
and the deer up ahead scatter, even one frozen in the middle of the road.


THE RADIO
---------
Put your own .mp3 or .ogg files in the "radio" folder next to HighwayOne.exe and the
car radio plays them, shuffled. N (or D-pad right) skips a song, B (or D-pad left)
turns the radio off and on.


COPS AND DEER
-------------
Sheriff's deputies sit on the shoulder just past crossroads. On Highway 1: one in
Washington County (Kalona up to the county line at mile 1.75), three in Johnson County.
On Highway 22 it's all Washington County: two deputies, and no county line. They move
every run. Go past one at over 72 mph, or hit one, and you've got a deputy on your tail.
  - Stop with the deputy right behind you for 3 seconds: BUSTED, the run's over.
    Calling the tow truck while they're after you counts as busted too.
  - Get 400 m ahead and stay there, or wreck the cruiser: lost the cop, 1,000.
    A deputy drives a little faster than your car can, so flooring it won't do it.
    Boosting will: two full boosts and they're gone.
  - The Washington County deputy stops at the county line: 2,000 if you drag them
    over it.
  - Keep it going for 2 and a half minutes and they give up: 1,000.

Deer cross near the creeks, and anywhere else now and then, a few at a time. If you
hold your speed and stay in your lane, they'll miss you, just. Speed up or swerve
and you might hit one: it hurts the car and it costs you the combo.

WHAT'S IN THIS BUILD
--------------------
v0.9.3: fixes. No more freezing for a moment by the woods (worst on Highway 22) or
hitching as the world loads, and an iPad gets a tablet's graphics settings, not a PC's.
The title screen's car can't set your records or post to the leaderboard any more,
bridges and the town streets don't kick up gravel dust, and typing your name doesn't
drive the car. Past the finish the road ends at a ROAD CLOSED barricade; keep your foot
on the gas after the finish and the results get out of the way (let off and they're
back), and on a phone the pedals stay up so you can drive on.

v0.9.2: the road no longer just stops at the finish: it carries on into Iowa City and
through Wellman, with curbs and street lights. Wellman's built up: downtown's storefronts
down 8th Ave (left at the finish), the water tower, and the co-op's grain elevators.
And the in-car view fits the new car bodies: no more gap between the dash and the hood.

v0.9.1: the browser version plays on phones and tablets, with touch controls. On a
touch-screen PC they come up here too as soon as you touch the screen (Settings, Touch
controls: Auto, On or Off).

v0.9: the game runs in a browser too, with a shared leaderboard of everyone's ten
fastest runs and ten best scores on each route (ask whoever sent you this for the
address). This download joins in once it's pointed at that site.

v0.8: a second route. On the garage's top line (the route), press Left or Right to
pick Highway 22: from the same 4-way stop in Kalona (you start in the middle of it,
pointed west), 7 miles over the English River and past Mid-Prairie High School to
downtown Wellman. It has its own records. Highway 1 is still there too. Both now have
real bridges over their creeks, guardrails and all (they're solid). And the cars look like
the real ones now: the Windstar, the Legend and the Tercel rebuilt after photos, with
proper windows, lights and wheels, in the photos' colors (the old ones are still in the
garage's paint list). And the four-way stop in Kalona looks like the real one: concrete,
left-turn lanes on IA 1 (on Highway 1 you start in the through lane, right of the turn
lane), a free right turn round every corner, light poles that come on at night, and the
KALONA welcome sign by the flagpole on the corner.

v0.7.2: sit behind the wheel. Press C (or Y) once for the in-car view: a dash with a
working speedometer, tachometer, fuel and temperature gauges, odometer and trip meter,
warning lights, and the radio's clock. Also, the tires no longer poke through the sides
of the cars: every car has proper wheel arches now.

v0.7.1: you can hear the engine now (it was there, but too low and too quiet for
laptop and TV speakers), and the tires: screeching in slides, hard stops and launches,
crunching on gravel, and the road and wind roaring louder the faster you go.

v0.7: the run now starts at the four-way stop in Kalona (stop signs on every corner, an
Amish buggy waiting its turn) and ends at the stop light at Mormon Trek Blvd in Iowa
City, 15.4 miles. The side roads and town streets are there now, paved in town and
gravel out in the country (you can drive down them), Kalona is lawns and houses
instead of corn, power lines run along the highway, the sky has clouds (and stars at
night), and tires leave skid marks, ruts and dust. The corn no longer pops in and out
in a wave as you drive.

v0.6: a title screen, a pause menu (Esc), settings for slow and fast PCs, a records
table, practice runs from any mile, crash and smash sounds, squealing tires, a horn
for every car, controller rumble, and a title for every run on the results screen.

v0.5.1: the boost (near misses fill it; it's how you lose a cop), and Kalona Cheese,
the old cheese factory and Kalona Creamery store at 540th St, 3.9 miles up. Don't
hit it: it's brick.

v0.5: our Highway 1. Every farmhouse, barn, machine shed, grain bin and tree line along
the road is where it really is (found in USGS lidar), the creeks and the county line
are signed with their real names, and there are five conditions to drive it in. Plus
the radio.

Before that: speed traps, chases and deer; the Windstar, the Tercel and the Legend,
each with its own handling, toughness, points and ways of falling apart; things to
smash, deep corn to plow, traffic both ways, Amish buggies and tractors to get around,
with real passing zones. The curves, hills, crossroads and street names come from USGS
map and elevation data, and you can see the real countryside out to about 3 km.

Running slow? Pick Low graphics in Settings, or press F2 to draw half the trees, or none.
