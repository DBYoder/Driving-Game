HIGHWAY 1: KALONA TO IOWA CITY
==============================

A homemade game about 15 real miles of Iowa Highway 1.


HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click HighwayOne.exe.
3. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC". That's because this is a
   homemade game without a paid code-signing certificate, not because anything is wrong.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only have to do this once per build.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Get from Kalona to Iowa City causing as much chaos as you can. The clock and the
scoring start when you cross the line at the IA-22 four-way stop in Kalona and stop
at the US-218 overpass. Your best score and best time are saved.

Points:
  Mailbox 50 · fence 25 · hay bale 100 (heavy: it hurts) · plowing corn 5 a meter
  Air time 100 per half second · near miss with oncoming traffic 250
  Passing a car 100 · a buggy 150 · a tractor 200 · a semi 300

Chain things together within 4 seconds and the combo multiplier climbs to x5. When
the combo runs out, its points are banked and safe. A hard hit, the tow truck, or a
wreck throws away the combo you haven't banked yet.

Make it to Iowa City and your whole score is multiplied by 1.5, plus 20 points for
every second under 15 minutes.

The run is over if:
  - the car is totaled (watch the health bar)
  - it's on its roof or side for 3 seconds
  - you hit an Amish buggy
  - you hit oncoming traffic head-on at speed
Stuck in a ditch? T calls the tow truck: back on the road for 500 points.


CONTROLS                      KEYBOARD            CONTROLLER
--------                      --------            ----------
Gas                           W or Up             Right trigger
Brake (hold to reverse)       S or Down           Left trigger
Steer                         A / D, Left/Right   Left stick
Handbrake                     Space               A
Change camera                 C                   Y
Tow truck (-500)              T                   X
Restart                       R                   Back
Pause                         P                   Start
Mute                          M
Quit                          Esc


WHAT'S IN THIS BUILD
--------------------
v0.2.1: one placeholder car on the real road, with things to smash, corn to plow,
traffic both ways, Amish buggies and tractors to get around, and real passing zones.
The curves, hills, crossroads and street names come from USGS map and elevation data,
and you can see the real countryside out to about 3 km.
Coming next: the Windstar, Tercel and Legend, then cops and deer.


IF IT WON'T START OR LOOKS BROKEN
---------------------------------
- Update your graphics driver.
- Tell whoever sent you this what kind of computer you have.
