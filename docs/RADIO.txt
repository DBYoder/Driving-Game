THE RADIO
=========

Drop your own .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder (radio/, next to HighwayOne.exe) and
the car radio plays them, shuffled, while you drive. Record your own fake local ads
and DJ bits too: they're just more songs.

    N or D-pad right    next song
    B or D-pad left     radio off / on
    M                   mute everything

Nothing here? The radio stays quiet.
